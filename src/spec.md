# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Create a marketing website for Beat o Beet cold pressed beetroot juice brand that showcases products, health benefits, and provides contact functionality.

**Planned changes:**
- Build hero section with Beat o Beet logo and natural vitality messaging
- Create product showcase displaying 4 beetroot juice variants in 180ml bottles (Pure Beetroot, Beetroot-Apple, Beetroot-Ginger, Beetroot-Carrot)
- Add "About Our Process" section explaining cold pressing method and 100% natural positioning
- Build "Health Benefits" section highlighting blood circulation, energy boost, antioxidants, and vitamins
- Implement "Where to Buy" contact form for wholesale inquiries
- Create responsive navigation with logo and section links
- Apply earthy, organic design theme with kraft paper aesthetic, warm earth tones (beige, tan, cream), beetroot red accents, and forest green secondary colors

**User-visible outcome:** Users can browse different beetroot juice variants, learn about cold pressing benefits and health advantages, and submit wholesale inquiries through a contact form.
